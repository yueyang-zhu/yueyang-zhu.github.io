PhD Student · School of Economics, Peking University

I am a PhD student in economic history at the School of Economics, Peking University. Before starting my PhD, I earned a bachelor's degree in economics from Peking University in 2023. My research interests are in economic history and political economy. My current research on imperial China examines civil service examinations and social mobility, as well as information and fiscal state capacity.

My work has appeared in the Journal of Peking University (Philosophy and Social Sciences). One of my working papers has received a revise-and-resubmit decision from the American Political Science Review. I have presented my research at the 9th and 12th International Symposia on Quantitative History. In 2025, I was named a Merit Student at Peking University and received a Yanbao Scholarship.
